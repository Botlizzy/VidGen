# VID-GEN design direction

## Chosen approach: Midnight Studio

### Design Movement
A contemporary editorial-tech direction inspired by broadcast control rooms, creator workspaces, and Swiss information design. The interface should feel precise and capable rather than sci-fi or ornamental.

### Core Principles
The design uses strong contrast, asymmetrical composition, visible process cues, and restrained motion. Large typographic statements are balanced by compact workflow modules so the product promise is instantly legible.

### Color Philosophy
Black and deep ink blue create a focused studio atmosphere; electric blue acts as the signal color for generation, automation, and action; white is reserved for clarity and confidence. The signature blue should feel like an active cursor moving through a creator’s pipeline, not a decorative gradient.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a split-stage landing page: an offset headline column paired with a working prompt console and a publishing status rail. Supporting sections should alternate between wide editorial bands and compact utility cards rather than repeating centered feature grids.

### Signature Elements
Use electric-blue connector lines and small status dots to suggest the automation graph. Use a clipped, high-contrast prompt console as the main visual anchor. Use monospaced metadata labels for workflow steps, platform names, and system states.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel immediate and instrument-like. Buttons respond with a small press scale and blue edge shift; navigation opens cleanly on mobile; auth forms show clear focus and validation without theatrical delays.

### Animation
Use short 160–240ms transform and opacity transitions with a snappy ease-out. On first load, reveal the hero copy and prompt console in a staggered sequence. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid animating layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Use Space Grotesk for headlines and Inter for body copy, with IBM Plex Mono for system labels and status metadata. Headlines use tight tracking and compact line-height; body copy stays readable at 16–18px; labels are uppercase with modest letter spacing.

### Brand Essence
VID-GEN is the prompt-to-publish studio for content creators who want more output without more tabs. Personality: direct, capable, energetic.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and active. CTAs sound like actions a creator would take, not generic onboarding language. Example lines: “One prompt. Every channel.” and “Turn the idea into a publishing run.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a compact angular play-arrow built from two offset blue bars, suggesting a video frame opening into motion. The wordmark is uppercase, wide, and tightly kerned, with a small blue cut between VID and GEN.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Blue: `#2D7DFF` — a clear, ownable action color that reads sharply against the midnight canvas and remains accessible with white type.

### Style Decisions
The landing page uses a dark foundation, bright blue action states, editorial asymmetry, and workflow-oriented microcopy. Avoid purple gradients, excessive pill shapes, fake testimonials, and generic “AI magic” language.
